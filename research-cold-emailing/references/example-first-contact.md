# Synthetic first-contact example

This example is fictional. It demonstrates how to move from a verified evidence record to an email without overstating either the professor's work or the student's preparation.

## Evidence record

```text
Professor: Professor Morgan Rivera
Institution and unit: Example University, Department of Economics
Official profile: fictional example only
Official email source: fictional example only
Research source title: Information Disclosures and Household Borrowing
Source type and date: fictional working-paper abstract, 2026
Exact supported detail: In the abstract, clearer fee disclosures reduce high-cost borrowing for first-time borrowers, but have little effect on repeat borrowers.
What was actually reviewed: abstract only
Currentness label: working paper
Student connection: Jordan is curious about financial decisions under incomplete information and has completed a Python-based applied statistics project.
Research fit: strong
Research-fit reason: The paper directly addresses Jordan's stated interest in financial decisions under incomplete information.
Current opportunity evidence: none found
Opportunity source and date: No current invitation or opening was found in the fictional source set.
Uncertainties or conflicts: The mechanism behind the difference between first-time and repeat borrowers is not stated in the abstract.
```

## Draft

**Subject:** `Undergraduate Research Inquiry — Household Finance`

```text
Dear Professor Rivera,

I'm Jordan Lee, a second-year undergraduate at Example University studying Economics and Statistics, and I'm writing to ask whether there might be opportunities to support your research.

I read the abstract of your working paper on fee disclosures and household borrowing. The difference between first-time and repeat borrowers caught my attention: clearer disclosures reduced high-cost borrowing for new borrowers but had little effect on repeat borrowers. It made me wonder whether experience changes how people use the information or whether repeat borrowers face constraints that disclosure cannot address.

In an applied statistics project, I used Python to clean a public rental dataset, document missing values, and estimate regression models. That experience gave me a starting point for careful data work, and I'd be interested in developing those skills in a research setting.

Would you be open to a brief conversation about your current work and whether undergraduate help could be useful?

Best,
Jordan Lee
Example University
Economics and Statistics
```

## Why it works

- It says the student read the abstract, not the full paper.
- It uses one supported finding.
- The student's question is framed as curiosity, not as a claim about the paper's mechanism.
- It includes one verified preparation example.
- The request can be answered briefly.
- It does not claim that Professor Rivera is recruiting.
- It uses exploratory wording because current opportunity evidence is `none found`.
